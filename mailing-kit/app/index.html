Notice & Mailing Kit: workspace

Your recipient list and your PDF stay in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or sent to us. Close the tab or press Reset and it is gone. This tool does not decide whom you must serve; your court's instructions do.

1Start with your court's guidance

Choose your bankruptcy court. If we have read that court's own page about the Bankruptcy Noticing Center disruption, its summary and link appear here. Otherwise you get the official website and a reminder to check it yourself.

2Choose what to print

Pick any combination. Packet copies need the original PDF from step 4. Labels and the checklist work with a recipient list alone.

3Add recipients

Paste address blocks separated by a blank line, open a CSV or text file from this device, or type one at a time. Review the list: blanks, possible duplicates, long lines, and missing ZIP codes are flagged, never removed or changed for you. Use each creditor's address exactly as your court's list or the creditor's notice gives it.

Headers recognized: name, address1, address2, city, state, zip. Without headers, each row's cells become the lines. Sample CSV

Recipients: 0 (edit any entry in place; up to 500 per run)

#Name and addressReview flags

4Add the document to copy (optional for labels only)

Open the court-issued notice or other original PDF from this device. Its pages are copied exactly as they are; nothing inside is edited, and no notice, case number, date, or address is ever generated for you. Password-protected PDFs are refused. Limit: 60 pages, 25 MB.

5Review and generate

    Print every file at 100% / "Actual size" with scaling off. For labels, print the test sheet on plain paper first and hold it against a label sheet. Downloading or printing does not mark anyone as served.

    6Record what you actually mailed

    Fill this in after the mail goes out. Every date starts blank; nothing is marked mailed by default. Export it as a CSV or as an unsigned worksheet PDF. It is not a certificate of service: if your court requires one, use the court's form or format and sign it yourself.
