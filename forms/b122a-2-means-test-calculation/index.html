Official Bankruptcy Form 122A-2 Means Test

Official Form 122A-2: Chapter 7 Means Test Calculation

The second means-test form, filed by a Chapter 7 debtor whose current monthly income on Form 122A-1 is above the state median for the household size.

Download Official Form 122A-2 (PDF, uscourts.gov)

Current version: updated April 1, 2025 (form marked 04/25; effective April 1, 2022), from uscourts.gov. Official form page.

What Form 122A-2 is

Official Form 122A-2 carries out the calculation often called the means test. The official instructions describe it as reducing income by living expenses and payments on certain debts, which leaves an amount available to pay other debts. If that amount is high enough, a presumption of abuse arises under Section 707(b) of the Bankruptcy Code.

The form starts from the filer's completed Official Form 122A-1. Line 1 copies total current monthly income from line 11 of that form.

Who must file it

Under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 1007(b)(4), an individual Chapter 7 debtor files Form 122A-1, and files Form 122A-2 only if current monthly income exceeds the median family income for the debtor's state and household size. The rule does not apply when Section 707(b)(2)(D) applies. The instructions explain that a filer covered by an exemption on Form 122A-1Supp (for example, debts that are not primarily consumer debts, or certain military service) completes only the verification on Form 122A-1.

The instructions also state that a filer under Chapter 11, 12, or 13 does not fill out the Chapter 7 means-test forms. Chapter 13 filers use Forms 122C-1 and 122C-2.

When it is due

Rule 1007(c)(1) requires the documents in Rule 1007(b)(4), which include Form 122A-2, to be filed with the petition or within 14 days after it is filed. The court may extend the time on motion and for cause under Rule 1007(c)(7).

How the form is organized

  • Part 1: Determine Your Adjusted Income. Starts from Form 122A-1 and allows a subtraction for any part of a non-filing spouse's income not regularly used for the household.
  • Part 2: Calculate Your Deductions from Your Income. Living expenses, secured debt payments, priority claims, and other allowed deductions.
  • Part 3: Determine Whether There Is a Presumption of Abuse. Compares the result to the thresholds printed on the form.
  • Part 4: Give Details About Special Circumstances. Where the filer may describe special circumstances that justify added expenses or income adjustments. The form states that the case trustee must also get documentation.
  • Part 5: Sign Below. A declaration under penalty of perjury.

Several lines need figures from outside the form. The instructions direct filers to justice.gov/ust/means-testing for lines 6 through 15, 30, and 36. The dollar thresholds in Part 3 carry a note on the form that they are subject to adjustment on April 1, 2028, and every 3 years after that.

What a presumption of abuse means

The official instructions state that a presumption of abuse does not mean the filer is actually trying to abuse the bankruptcy system. It means the filer is presumed to have enough income that Chapter 7 relief should not be granted. The instructions add that the presumption may be overcome by showing special circumstances, and that a filer who cannot get Chapter 7 relief may be eligible under another chapter. They also note that, depending on the result, the U.S. trustee, bankruptcy administrator, or creditors can file a motion to dismiss under Section 707(b).

Common problems the form and instructions point to

  • Starting without Form 122A-1. The form states that a completed Form 122A-1 is needed to fill it out.
  • An outdated version. The current form was updated April 1, 2025, and its thresholds change on the schedule printed on the form.
  • Missing outside figures. Lines that depend on the U.S. Trustee Program's means-testing data cannot be completed from the form alone.
  • Special circumstances without support. Part 4 states that documentation goes to the case trustee.
  • No signature. Part 5 is a declaration under penalty of perjury.

Frequently asked questions

Who has to fill out Form 122A-2?
Under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 1007(b)(4), an individual Chapter 7 debtor files Form 122A-2 when current monthly income on Form 122A-1 exceeds the median family income for the debtor's state and household size, unless Section 707(b)(2)(D) applies.
When is Form 122A-2 due?
Rule 1007(c)(1) requires it with the petition or within 14 days after the petition is filed. The court may extend the time on motion and for cause.
Does a presumption of abuse mean the filer did something wrong?
No. The official instructions state that a presumption of abuse does not mean the filer is actually trying to abuse the bankruptcy system, and that it may be overcome by showing special circumstances.
