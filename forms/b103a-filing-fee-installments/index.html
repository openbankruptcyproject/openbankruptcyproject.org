Official Bankruptcy Form 103A

Official Form 103A: Application for Individuals to Pay the Filing Fee in Installments

The form an individual files with the petition to ask the court for permission to pay the bankruptcy filing fee in up to four payments instead of all at once.

Download Official Form 103A (PDF, uscourts.gov)

Current version: effective December 1, 2015 (form marked 12/15), from uscourts.gov. Official form page.

What Form 103A is

Official Form 103A is an application to pay the bankruptcy filing fee in installments. Under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 1006(b)(1), the clerk must accept an individual's voluntary petition for filing, even if no part of the fee is paid, when the petition comes with a completed and signed Form 103A.

The form has two parts. Part 1 is the proposed payment timetable. Part 2 is the signature, where the filer states that they cannot pay the full fee at once. The form also includes a proposed order that the court may use, change, or replace with its own.

Who files it

Individuals file Form 103A. Line 1 of the form covers Chapter 7, 11, 12, and 13 cases. The official instructions state that the form is not meant for a filer who can afford to pay the full fee at filing. A Chapter 7 filer who cannot pay the fee at all, even in installments, may qualify to ask for a waiver on Official Form 103B.

When it is filed

The official instructions list Form 103A among the documents filed on the date the case is opened, alongside the petition, when the filer cannot pay the entire fee.

The payment limits in Rule 1006

  • No more than four installments. The court designates the number of installments, which may not exceed four, along with the amount and date of each.
  • 120 days. All payments must be made within 120 days after the petition is filed.
  • Extension up to 180 days. The court may extend the time for an installment for cause, but the last one must be paid within 180 days after filing.
  • Court decides the timetable. Before the meeting of creditors, the court may order payment of the whole fee or set installments. The form states that if the court approves the application, the court will set the final payment timetable.

Filing fee amounts

The "entire fee" the installments must add up to depends on the chapter. These totals match the fees printed in the official instructions:

Chapter 7: $338

$245 filing fee under 28 U.S.C. Section 1930(a)(1)(A), plus the $78 administrative fee and the $15 trustee fee set by the Judicial Conference's miscellaneous fee schedule.

Chapter 13: $313

$235 filing fee under 28 U.S.C. Section 1930(a)(1)(B), plus the $78 administrative fee.

Chapter 12: $278

$200 filing fee under 28 U.S.C. Section 1930(a)(5), plus the $78 administrative fee.

Chapter 11: $1,738

$1,167 filing fee under 28 U.S.C. Section 1930(a)(3), plus the $571 administrative fee.

What attaches to it

  • If a bankruptcy petition preparer helped complete the form, the instructions call for the preparer's Notice, Declaration, and Signature (Official Form 119) to be included.
  • The last page of the form is the court's order approving installment payments, which the court fills in.

Common problems the form and rule point to

  • Proposed payments that do not add up. The form states that the total must equal the entire fee for the chapter checked on line 1.
  • A timetable outside the rule. More than four installments, or a final payment later than 120 days after filing, falls outside Rule 1006(b)(2) unless the court extends it.
  • An unsigned or incomplete application. Rule 1006(b)(1) requires a completed and signed application for the clerk to accept a petition without the fee.
  • Paying others first. Until the fee is paid in full, Rule 1006(b)(3) bars further payment to an attorney or anyone else who provides services in connection with the case.
  • A missed installment. The form warns that if a payment is not made when due, the case may be dismissed. It also states that debts will not be discharged until the entire fee is paid.

Frequently asked questions

How many installments can the bankruptcy filing fee be split into?
Under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 1006(b)(2), the court may allow no more than four installments, and all payments must be made within 120 days after the petition is filed. For cause, the court may extend an installment, but the last one must be paid within 180 days after filing.
Can debts be discharged before the filing fee is paid?
Official Form 103A states that debts will not be discharged until the entire filing fee is paid.
Where is the official Form 103A?
The current Official Form 103A, effective December 1, 2015, is free on uscourts.gov. This page links directly to that PDF.
